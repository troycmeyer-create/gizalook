How it works

Three steps.
That's the whole thing.

No training courses. No 90-day onboarding. If you can upload a PDF, you can use Gizalook. Workers are reading steps on their first day.

📤

Upload the drawing

Supervisor uploads the PDF. Selects the experience level of the crew — Year 1 apprentice through to senior tradesman. Hits Generate. Gizalook reads the drawing and creates plain-language steps, critical numbers, materials, and hold points automatically.

✅

Review and publish

Supervisor checks the steps before they go live. Anything that needs a tweak — add a field note. Hit Publish and the steps appear on every worker's phone instantly. One drawing. Every worker. Same understanding.

📱

Workers check off steps on site

Big text. Simple language. One step at a time. Tick it off, move to the next. VERIFY flags show in orange — can't be skipped. Supervisor sees real-time progress across every job, every worker. When the plans change, push a field note. Workers see it before the next step.

Adapts to your crew

Same drawing.
Four different views.

A first-year apprentice and a 10-year leading hand don't need the same level of detail. Gizalook adjusts the language, depth, and context for the person holding the phone.

Year 1–2

The Apprentice

Full knowledge layer. Every abbreviation explained. Every step with context. Learns while working — builds plan literacy on the job, not instead of it.

Year 3–4

The Intermediate

Clear instructions with key context. Dimensions and materials front and centre. Ambiguous details flagged for verification. Gets it done right without the noise.

Qualified

The Tradesman

Condensed view. Key steps and critical tolerances. Enough detail to get it right, not so much it slows you down. Confirms what you already know.

Senior

The Leading Hand

Exception-based. Only what's unusual on this drawing. Flags, deviations, hold points. Everything non-standard surfaces automatically.
